Objective 01

Understand electrode potential

Standard reduction potential (E°) measures the tendency of a species to gain electrons under standard conditions.

Mn+ + ne− → M
Objective 02

Predict redox feasibility

Compare two standard reduction potentials. The higher value is the cathode (reduction); the lower value is reversed for oxidation at the anode.

E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode
Objective 03

Explore the Nernst equation

See how concentration and temperature affect cell potential away from standard conditions.

E = E° − (0.0592/n) log Q

At 298 K, for a reaction involving n electrons.

Nernst equation virtual experiment

For the model cell Zn(s) + Cu²⁺(aq) → Zn²⁺(aq) + Cu(s), E°cell = +1.10 V and n = 2.

E = 1.10 − (0.0592 / 2) log₁₀([Zn²⁺] / [Cu²⁺])
At 1 M concentrations, Q = 1 and E = E° = +1.10 V.
